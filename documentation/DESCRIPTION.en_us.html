Molander is a casual handwritten typeface drawn by Jab Bonstein. Every letter was traced from a single sheet of pen-on-paper writing, keeping the slight wobble, uneven proportions and quick strokes of real handwriting, while evening out the stroke weight so text reads consistently at any size.

Capitals stand noticeably taller than the lowercase, as they do in natural handwriting. The family covers the Google Fonts Latin Core character set, with accented letters for most Western and Central European languages built from the original pen strokes.

To contribute, see github.com/Jab-Bonstein/molander.
